How to use it

Sit an arm's length from the screen, letters at or just below eye level, room light on. Do the eye warm-up, then eight to ten minutes of drilling, then stop. Two short sessions a day beat one long one.

Three taps to start: pick a list, pick a speed, pick a style. Tap the middle of the reading area whenever a word feels hard — it gets saved for you.

Grow slowly. Stay on 1x until words feel easy, then 1.5x, then 2x. Move to the letter methods only when whole words at 3x feel comfortable.

Stop if you get dry eyes, headache or blurring, and rest. Drills should feel like effort, never strain. If blurring or headaches keep coming back, get an eye test — a small uncorrected focus error is the most common reason reading feels slow.
